2. Content to develop, section by section

1. Opening and fit

A clear H1 and 60–90 words explaining the role of a Seychelles structure without claiming that the country is universally suitable.

2. When this jurisdiction may be relevant

Use 2–3 carefully qualified scenarios grounded in verified facts. Include circumstances needing extra assessment rather than presenting only benefits.

3. Decisions before formation

Address the jurisdiction-specific research questions below. Distinguish entity formation, licensing, tax and practical operating requirements.

4. Circulox scope and deliverables

State exactly what the firm advises on, coordinates and delivers; identify any external provider roles and exclusions.

5. Documents and process

Describe owner-confirmed client inputs and steps. State official requirements only after checking current primary sources. Leave unconfirmed cost and timing as questions.

6. After formation

Explain ongoing corporate, tax, recordkeeping and operational topics that need assessment. Do not imply everything is included in the formation engagement.

7. Questions and next step

Provide 4–6 country-specific FAQs and one final booking action. Link back to broader formation advice and relevant services.
