The firm

Existing public page · /firm

This is a writing brief, not a redesign or approved legal advice. Proposed additions are requests for content, not features or services already confirmed.

1. What this page needs to do

A prospective client checks who is behind the advice and whether the firm is credible for their situation.

This page needs real people, confirmed credentials and a concrete engagement approach. The current page has an approach section but no actual team biographies.

Length guidance: Approximately 500–800 words, excluding optional individual biographies.

2. Content to develop, section by section

1. Opening

Explain the firm and the business problems it addresses in 50–80 words; avoid duplicating the homepage introduction verbatim.

2. How we work

Describe the owner's real process for understanding a matter, defining scope, involving specialists and communicating advice. Mark unconfirmed steps.

3. Team — proposed addition

Request approved names, roles, portraits, biographies, qualifications and relevant jurisdictional admissions. Write 80–120 words per confirmed person. This is missing content, not an existing populated team section.

4. Cross-border delivery

Explain directly delivered work versus coordination with external/local advisers, only as confirmed. Do not imply offices or admissions in each country.

5. Evidence and next step

Use approved matter examples or credentials if supplied. Link naturally to relevant expertise and Contact; do not invent awards or numbers.

3. Questions for the owner

  • What legal entity and professional status should be disclosed?
  • Who may be named, and what qualifications/admissions can be verified?
  • How is local advice obtained where needed?
  • What real examples or distinctions are approved for publication?

Record answers in a separate document or paste them into the prompt below. This HTML is a portable reference file; it does not save edits.

4. Search and internal links

Suggested intent: about Circulox legal and business advisory. This is an editorial suggestion, not researched keyword-volume data.

Keep the existing route. Provide one H1, useful H2s, a distinct search title and description. Do not repeat country pages with only the country name changed.
