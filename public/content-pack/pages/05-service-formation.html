Company incorporation & international structuring

Existing service page · /services/formation

This is a writing brief, not a redesign or approved legal advice. Proposed additions are requests for content, not features or services already confirmed.

1. What this page needs to do

A founder needs to choose where and how to register a company, not buy an unexplained incorporation package.

Explain jurisdiction selection before filing: activity, founder residence, customer markets, investors, banking and group ownership.

Length guidance: Approximately 650–950 words of useful copy; provide a short 35–50 word mobile summary separately.

2. Content to develop, section by section

1. Opening and service fit

Explain jurisdiction selection before filing: activity, founder residence, customer markets, investors, banking and group ownership. Write one H1 and a 50–80 word introduction identifying the visitor's problem and the confirmed support.

2. Company formation

Formation support across the US, UK, UAE, Singapore, India, BVI, Cayman and Seychelles, with jurisdiction-specific scope discussed for your business. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

3. Holding companies & SPVs

Structures for ownership, investment and defined business activities, considered alongside your wider group. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

4. International structuring

Jurisdiction advisory and offshore structuring, with corporate, banking and operational requirements considered together. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

5. Engagement process — proposed addition

Request the real steps from first discussion to delivery, client inputs, exclusions and responsibility boundaries. Write 3–4 short steps only after confirmation.

6. Before we speak

Retain the three current preparation points; make each specific and concise. Do not request confidential documents in a public form.

7. Questions

Answer 4–6 substantive questions, approximately 40–70 words each, avoiding repeated promotional sentences.

8. Related expertise and next step

Use contextual related-service links and one final booking action. Do not add a contact button to every scope item.

Page-specific points to address

  • Distinguish operating companies, holding companies and special-purpose vehicles in plain language.
  • Explain the advisory-to-formation handoff, the documents produced and post-formation support, only after the owner confirms the scope.
  • Give a decision framework for the eight supported jurisdictions, with links to their individual guides rather than a universal 'best country'.

3. Questions for the owner

  • Which entity types and countries do you actually handle, directly or through local providers?
  • What is included after registration, and what is separately quoted?
  • Can you provide an approved anonymised example of how a structure was selected?

Record answers in a separate document or paste them into the prompt below. This HTML is a portable reference file; it does not save edits.

Useful FAQ questions

  • How do you help us choose a jurisdiction?
  • Can non-resident founders engage you?
  • What documents should we prepare?
  • Are banking and tax advice included?
  • Can you restructure an existing company?
