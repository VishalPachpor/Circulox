Contact

Existing public page · /contact

This is a writing brief, not a redesign or approved legal advice. Proposed additions are requests for content, not features or services already confirmed.

1. What this page needs to do

An interested visitor wants a straightforward way to book a relevant conversation or send a short enquiry.

Keep booking primary, email secondary and the preparation guidance brief. Do not repeat the same action after every paragraph.

Length guidance: Approximately 180–300 words; preparation guidance should fit a short phone screen.

2. Content to develop, section by section

1. Opening

A clear H1 and 35–60 words explaining what to share. Existing action: Book a consultation; alternative: iptisha@circulox.org.

2. Before we speak

Three concise preparation points: business/activity, countries involved, main question and timing. Avoid asking for sensitive documents before an appropriate intake process.

3. What happens next — proposed copy

Explain call purpose, participants, duration or fee only if the owner confirms each point. Avoid a guaranteed response time.

4. Enquiry form — future specification

The current page has booking/email, not a submitted enquiry form. If requested, define name, work email, company, matter category, jurisdiction and brief message, plus truthful privacy/consent and success/error copy. Do not describe this as already implemented.

3. Questions for the owner

  • Is the call paid or free, and is any wording about this approved?
  • What is the confirmed duration and who attends?
  • What intake/confidentiality wording has the firm approved?
  • Should a form be built later, and who will receive enquiries?

Record answers in a separate document or paste them into the prompt below. This HTML is a portable reference file; it does not save edits.

4. Search and internal links

Suggested intent: contact Circulox consultation. This is an editorial suggestion, not researched keyword-volume data.

Keep the existing route. Provide one H1, useful H2s, a distinct search title and description. Do not repeat country pages with only the country name changed.
