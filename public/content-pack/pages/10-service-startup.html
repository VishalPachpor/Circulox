Startup & investment advisory

Existing service page · /services/startup

This is a writing brief, not a redesign or approved legal advice. Proposed additions are requests for content, not features or services already confirmed.

1. What this page needs to do

A founder is preparing a round or aligning equity, investor terms and company structure.

Begin with the funding stage and transaction, then connect the documents to ownership and governance.

Length guidance: Approximately 650–950 words of useful copy; provide a short 35–50 word mobile summary separately.

2. Content to develop, section by section

1. Opening and service fit

Begin with the funding stage and transaction, then connect the documents to ownership and governance. Write one H1 and a 50–80 word introduction identifying the visitor's problem and the confirmed support.

2. Fundraising documentation

Support with fundraising documents, including SAFE and SAFT agreements, in the context of the proposed round. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

3. Equity structuring

Advice on equity structures and ownership arrangements for the company and its stakeholders. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

4. Investment vehicles & support

Investment vehicles and continuing startup legal support as the business develops. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

5. Engagement process — proposed addition

Request the real steps from first discussion to delivery, client inputs, exclusions and responsibility boundaries. Write 3–4 short steps only after confirmation.

6. Before we speak

Retain the three current preparation points; make each specific and concise. Do not request confidential documents in a public form.

7. Questions

Answer 4–6 substantive questions, approximately 40–70 words each, avoiding repeated promotional sentences.

8. Related expertise and next step

Use contextual related-service links and one final booking action. Do not add a contact button to every scope item.

Page-specific points to address

  • Describe fundraising documents, equity structuring and investment vehicles as separate workstreams.
  • Explain SAFE and SAFT at a high level with a legal-review flag for suitability; do not present them as universally interchangeable.
  • Show the information required before advising: cap table, current agreements, investor terms and company jurisdictions.

3. Questions for the owner

  • Which round stages, instruments and jurisdictions do you handle?
  • Do you negotiate investor documents or support only drafting/review?
  • What continuing legal support is available after a round?

Record answers in a separate document or paste them into the prompt below. This HTML is a portable reference file; it does not save edits.

Useful FAQ questions

  • When should legal work start during a fundraise?
  • Can you review a SAFE or SAFT?
  • Can you help with an existing cap table?
  • Can the round involve overseas investors?
  • Is continuing startup support available?
