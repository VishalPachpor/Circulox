Legal notices & dispute resolution

Existing service page · /services/disputes

This is a writing brief, not a redesign or approved legal advice. Proposed additions are requests for content, not features or services already confirmed.

1. What this page needs to do

An operator has received a notice, encountered a platform dispute or needs advice before escalation.

Prioritise the issue, documents, deadlines and desired outcome; describe a measured assessment before action.

Length guidance: Approximately 650–950 words of useful copy; provide a short 35–50 word mobile summary separately.

2. Content to develop, section by section

1. Opening and service fit

Prioritise the issue, documents, deadlines and desired outcome; describe a measured assessment before action. Write one H1 and a 50–80 word introduction identifying the visitor's problem and the confirmed support.

2. Legal notices

Advice and support concerning legal notices and the correspondence surrounding a business dispute. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

3. Platform disputes

Support with platform disputes and regulatory representations, shaped around the issue and relevant documents. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

4. Pre-litigation advisory

Advice before litigation, considering the specific matter, available information and the business context. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

5. Engagement process — proposed addition

Request the real steps from first discussion to delivery, client inputs, exclusions and responsibility boundaries. Write 3–4 short steps only after confirmation.

6. Before we speak

Retain the three current preparation points; make each specific and concise. Do not request confidential documents in a public form.

7. Questions

Answer 4–6 substantive questions, approximately 40–70 words each, avoiding repeated promotional sentences.

8. Related expertise and next step

Use contextual related-service links and one final booking action. Do not add a contact button to every scope item.

Page-specific points to address

  • Differentiate drafting a notice, responding to a notice, platform disputes and pre-litigation advice.
  • Clarify the firm's representation boundary and whether court proceedings require separately engaged local counsel.
  • Explain how to prepare a chronology and preserve correspondence. Do not offer a public upload of confidential documents or promise a recovery.

3. Questions for the owner

  • Which forums and jurisdictions can you act in, and in what capacity?
  • Does the scope include court proceedings, negotiation, or pre-litigation work only?
  • How should a client flag a real deadline without assuming emergency availability?

Record answers in a separate document or paste them into the prompt below. This HTML is a portable reference file; it does not save edits.

Useful FAQ questions

  • What should I do before an initial discussion?
  • Can you help respond to a legal notice?
  • Do you handle platform disputes?
  • Does this include court representation?
  • How should I flag a deadline?
