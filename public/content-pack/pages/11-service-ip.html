Intellectual property

Existing service page · /services/ip

This is a writing brief, not a redesign or approved legal advice. Proposed additions are requests for content, not features or services already confirmed.

1. What this page needs to do

A founder needs to protect a brand or clarify ownership and commercial use of IP.

Separate trademark registration, copyright/brand protection and IP ownership or licensing agreements.

Length guidance: Approximately 650–950 words of useful copy; provide a short 35–50 word mobile summary separately.

2. Content to develop, section by section

1. Opening and service fit

Separate trademark registration, copyright/brand protection and IP ownership or licensing agreements. Write one H1 and a 50–80 word introduction identifying the visitor's problem and the confirmed support.

2. Trademark registration

Support with trademark registration for the brand and the jurisdictions relevant to the business. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

3. Copyright & brand protection

Advice concerning copyright and the protection of business identity and creative work. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

4. IP agreements

Intellectual property agreements connected to ownership, use and commercial relationships. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

5. Engagement process — proposed addition

Request the real steps from first discussion to delivery, client inputs, exclusions and responsibility boundaries. Write 3–4 short steps only after confirmation.

6. Before we speak

Retain the three current preparation points; make each specific and concise. Do not request confidential documents in a public form.

7. Questions

Answer 4–6 substantive questions, approximately 40–70 words each, avoiding repeated promotional sentences.

8. Related expertise and next step

Use contextual related-service links and one final booking action. Do not add a contact button to every scope item.

Page-specific points to address

  • Explain that the protection needed depends on the asset, market and ownership; avoid calling a filing 'global protection'.
  • Describe clearance, filing, examination or objection support only if the owner confirms these stages are included.
  • Connect founder, employee and contractor IP ownership to agreements; avoid offering an unsupported guarantee of registration.

3. Questions for the owner

  • Which jurisdictions and filing stages do you cover?
  • Are searches, objections, oppositions and renewals within scope or separate?
  • Do you handle assignments and licensing agreements as well as trademark work?

Record answers in a separate document or paste them into the prompt below. This HTML is a portable reference file; it does not save edits.

Useful FAQ questions

  • Which markets should a trademark application cover?
  • What brand information do you need?
  • Can you review existing IP ownership agreements?
  • Can you help with a trademark objection?
  • Does filing guarantee registration?
