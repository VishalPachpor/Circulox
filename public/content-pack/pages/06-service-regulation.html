1. What this page needs to do

A product founder or compliance lead needs to understand regulatory exposure before launching or expanding.

Start with product mechanics, custody, asset flows, customer locations and the exact regulatory question.

Length guidance: Approximately 650–950 words of useful copy; provide a short 35–50 word mobile summary separately.

2. Content to develop, section by section

1. Opening and service fit

Start with product mechanics, custody, asset flows, customer locations and the exact regulatory question. Write one H1 and a 50–80 word introduction identifying the visitor's problem and the confirmed support.

2. Regulatory scope & legal opinions

Advice concerning crypto, fintech, DeFi, exchanges, derivatives and prediction markets, including legal opinions where appropriate to the engagement. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

3. VASP licensing

Licensing advisory shaped around the proposed activities and relevant jurisdictions. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

4. AML/KYC & compliance

Advice on anti-money laundering, customer verification and regulatory compliance for the business and its operating model. Expand only with owner-confirmed deliverables and useful decision context; approximately 60–100 words.

5. Engagement process — proposed addition

Request the real steps from first discussion to delivery, client inputs, exclusions and responsibility boundaries. Write 3–4 short steps only after confirmation.

6. Before we speak

Retain the three current preparation points; make each specific and concise. Do not request confidential documents in a public form.

7. Questions

Answer 4–6 substantive questions, approximately 40–70 words each, avoiding repeated promotional sentences.

8. Related expertise and next step

Use contextual related-service links and one final booking action. Do not add a contact button to every scope item.

Page-specific points to address

  • Differentiate a regulatory assessment, a legal opinion, licensing support and ongoing compliance work.
  • Describe DeFi, exchanges, derivatives and prediction markets as matters to assess, without claiming that all models are lawful in all markets.
  • Explain what an AML/KYC engagement can deliver and what the client's compliance team remains responsible for.

3. Questions for the owner

  • Which regulators, licences and jurisdictions are within your confirmed experience?
  • Do you prepare applications, provide opinions, coordinate local counsel, or a combination?
  • What facts and reliance limits are needed before you issue a legal opinion?

Record answers in a separate document or paste them into the prompt below. This HTML is a portable reference file; it does not save edits.

Useful FAQ questions

  • When should we seek a regulatory assessment?
  • What does a legal opinion cover?
  • Can you assist with VASP licensing?
  • What product and asset-flow information do you need?
  • Can you support an already launched product?
