1. What this page needs to do

A reader wants a clear answer to one specific business/legal question, supported by reviewed sources and a named author.

Commission an original article, not a generic SEO essay. Choose one issue, one audience and the relevant jurisdiction(s). No sample article should be represented as published.

Length guidance: Usually 900–1,400 words where the question justifies it; shorter is appropriate for a focused update.

2. Content to develop, section by section

1. Title and excerpt

Title at most 150 characters; excerpt at most 280 characters. Answer the reader's question directly in the introduction.

2. Useful explanation

Use H2/H3 sections with examples marked hypothetical. Explain applicability, decision factors, practical steps and limitations. Cite official sources for legal claims.

3. Author, dates and sources

Request a real author, role, reviewer and approval date. Identify the law/source review date separately from the publication date; never invent a reviewer.

4. Search and related reading

Provide one permitted CMS category, 3–6 useful tags, proposed slug and 2–3 relevant internal links. Search title at most 70 characters and search description at most 170.

5. Images

If needed, propose a relevant image brief, accurate alt text and credit/permission information. Do not fabricate a case photo or author portrait.

6. Ending

Use a helpful next step and the existing general-information note; add a service link when relevant rather than multiple booking buttons.

Page-specific points to address

  • CMS categories: Corporate structuring; Regulation & fintech; Technology compliance; Governance & contracts; Intellectual property; Banking & tax; Startup & investment; Dispute resolution.
  • CMS fields: title, slug, excerpt, author, category, tags (at most 12), optional cover with alt/caption, body, publication date, optional SEO title and description.

Write for Circulox, an international legal and business advisory firm serving startups, fintech, Web3 and established enterprises. The business goal is a relevant consultation, reached through clear information and credible evidence. Use calm, specific English, short paragraphs and useful headings. Keep the premium editorial tone; avoid hype, generic slogans, emojis, repeated contact buttons and keyword stuffing.
Separate supplied facts from proposed wording. Existing website copy is a draft, not independent proof of a legal rule or an achievement. Never invent offices, admissions, team qualifications, client engagements, case results, testimonials, success rates, years in business, partnerships, turnaround times, prices, tax benefits or guaranteed approvals. Client logos are approved for display; they do not establish the scope or outcome of any engagement. Do not generate endorsements from that list.
For current laws, filing requirements, licensing, tax, costs or deadlines, use dated primary official sources and identify the relevant jurisdiction. If browsing or confirmation is unavailable, use [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: question] instead of guessing. Separate legal requirements from Circulox's own process. Do not imply that incorporation authorises regulated activity or guarantees a bank account. Flag material claims for the firm's legal review.
Use only one primary booking destination: https://calendly.com/iptisha. Email alternative: iptisha@circulox.org. Do not call the consultation free or promise a response time without approval. Do not create login, editor or admin navigation in public copy. Keep substantive detail useful on desktop, with a concise summary first for phone readers.
