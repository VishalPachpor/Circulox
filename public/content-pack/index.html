20 existing public pages + 1 future article template. Each HTML file opens independently, includes the current draft copy and contains a tailored prompt for GPT.

How to use this pack

  1. Choose a page below and upload that HTML file to GPT. You can also copy its complete prompt.
  2. Answer the owner questions and ask GPT to draft content section by section. Unknown facts must remain marked for confirmation.
  3. Return the reviewed copy with its page route and section IDs. The Circulox owner approves business facts and the appropriate reviewer approves legal claims before publishing.

The files work offline without fonts, images, scripts or a login. Browser print/save as PDF also works. These are content briefs, not editable CMS pages; no draft changes are saved in the HTML.

Scope follows the current site. The Firm brief includes a proposed team section; there is no separate populated Team page. Privacy, terms and any future pages need separately approved briefs. The article file is a reusable writing template, not an existing published article.

Master writing rules

Write for Circulox, an international legal and business advisory firm serving startups, fintech, Web3 and established enterprises. The business goal is a relevant consultation, reached through clear information and credible evidence. Use calm, specific English, short paragraphs and useful headings. Keep the premium editorial tone; avoid hype, generic slogans, emojis, repeated contact buttons and keyword stuffing.
Separate supplied facts from proposed wording. Existing website copy is a draft, not independent proof of a legal rule or an achievement. Never invent offices, admissions, team qualifications, client engagements, case results, testimonials, success rates, years in business, partnerships, turnaround times, prices, tax benefits or guaranteed approvals. Client logos are approved for display; they do not establish the scope or outcome of any engagement. Do not generate endorsements from that list.
For current laws, filing requirements, licensing, tax, costs or deadlines, use dated primary official sources and identify the relevant jurisdiction. If browsing or confirmation is unavailable, use [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: question] instead of guessing. Separate legal requirements from Circulox's own process. Do not imply that incorporation authorises regulated activity or guarantees a bank account. Flag material claims for the firm's legal review.
Use only one primary booking destination: https://calendly.com/iptisha. Email alternative: iptisha@circulox.org. Do not call the consultation free or promise a response time without approval. Do not create login, editor or admin navigation in public copy. Keep substantive detail useful on desktop, with a concise summary first for phone readers. Put longer secondary material in FAQs or future guides; do not turn every section into a list.
