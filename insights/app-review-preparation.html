Preparing for a financial app review

A practical way to organise product details and review correspondence before seeking advice.

Keep the original correspondence

Retain the complete rejection notice, review history and any follow-up correspondence. Note the app version and features involved so the discussion can address the issue actually raised.

Explain the user journey

Outline what a user can do in the app, the countries it serves and how payments or assets move through the product. Screenshots and a short journey diagram can make the explanation easier to follow.

Connect the app to the business

Have the company details and relevant product documents available. Explain who operates the service and which agreements or legal documents users see.

Separate the questions

Identify the platform-policy question, the product-design question and any legal or regulatory questions you want to discuss. This is a preparation checklist, not a prediction of a review outcome.
