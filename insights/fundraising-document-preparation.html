Before a funding round: organise the legal picture

A focused starting brief for conversations about investment, equity and company documents.

Start with the current ownership

Gather the current cap table and the company documents that describe existing ownership arrangements. Include any prior investment agreements that may be relevant to a new round.

Describe the proposed round

Set out the intended investment, proposed instrument and terms already under discussion. Mark which points are agreed, which are proposals and which remain open.

Collect the documents already in circulation

Keep one organised set of drafts, correspondence and existing agreements. A clear version history helps advisers understand the current position without relying on conflicting copies.

Frame the next conversation

Explain the commercial objective, the decision you need to make and any stated deadlines. This helps define the questions for fundraising documentation, equity structuring and ongoing legal support.
